Delete your account

Last updated 28 October 2026

This page is for Liftbound, published by Zsombor Toreky.

In the app

  1. Open Liftbound and sign in.
  2. Go to your profile and open Settings.
  3. Under Account, choose Delete account.
  4. Enter your password (or type DELETE if you signed in with Google or Apple) and confirm.

Your account is deleted at once. You are signed out, and the copy of your log on your phone is erased.

If you signed in with Apple, Apple’s own sheet opens once on an iPhone. Confirm it there, so that your sign-in is withdrawn from your Apple ID. If you close it, your account is still deleted. On another device, the app can be removed from the list in your Apple ID settings (Sign-In & Security, Sign in with Apple).

Without the app

If you cannot open the app, write to liftbound [dot] support [at] gmail [dot] com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm that the request is yours, delete the account within 30 days and tell you when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, your email address and your sign-in.
  • Your profile, handle, display name, date of birth and settings.
  • Every workout, plan, exercise, set and note you logged, and your bodyweight readings, steps, water and food, including the foods you saved.
  • Your follows, followers, likes, comments, the accounts you blocked, badges and league history.
  • Reports you made in the app, and reports others made about you.

If you added a profile picture, its file is deleted from storage within 24 hours of the account being deleted.

What we keep, and for how long

  • Backups held by our hosting provider keep a copy until they expire, within [[backup retention in days]] days. They are not used to restore a deleted account.
  • Emails you sent us (including a report sent by email) and copyright notices that mention your handle, for as long as needed to deal with them and no longer than three years, where we need them to defend or enforce our rights.
  • Anything the law requires us to keep.
