AI features

Last updated 12 November 2026

Some parts of Liftbound use an AI model. This page says what it does for you, what it cannot do, and what happens to what you tell it.

What it does

You can describe a meal and get an itemised estimate of its calories to check, change and log, and describe a workout and get a plan to check and save. Each one is a single request: the AI can send you one message of questions back, then it answers.

  • You are always told when you are talking to an AI and not to a person.
  • It is offered to everyone who can use Liftbound, which is people aged 16 and over.

What it cannot do

The AI is not a doctor, a physiotherapist, a dietitian or a counsellor, and it is not an emergency service.
  • It can be wrong, and it sounds sure when it is. Check anything that matters.
  • It does not know your medical history, your injuries or how you are feeling.
  • It will not give advice on very low calorie diets, losing weight fast, supplements or drugs, or training through pain. It will tell you to see a professional.
  • Do not use it for an emergency. Call your local emergency number.

If you are struggling

If you tell the AI that you are thinking about harming yourself, or describe an eating disorder, it stops coaching and shows you where to get help. It does not argue with you or carry on as normal.

If you are in danger now, call your local emergency number. Free and confidential help:

  • Switzerland: Emergency, 144 for an ambulance, 117 for the police, or 112; Die Dargebotene Hand, 143; Pro Juventute, 147.
  • Germany: Emergency, 112; Telefonseelsorge, 0800 111 0 111 or 0800 111 0 222; Nummer gegen Kummer, 116 111.
  • Austria: Emergency, 112; Telefonseelsorge, 142; Rat auf Draht, 147.
  • Hungary: Emergency, 112; Lelki Elsősegély Telefonszolgálat, 116 123; Kék Vonal Gyermekkrízis Alapítvány, 116 111.
  • France: Emergency, 112; Numéro national de prévention du suicide, 3114.
  • United Kingdom: Emergency, 999; Samaritans, 116 123; Shout, Text SHOUT to 85258; Childline, 0800 1111.
  • Ireland: Emergency, 999 or 112; Samaritans, 116 123; Pieta, 1800 247 247.
  • United States: Emergency, 911; 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, Call or text 988.
  • Anywhere else: findahelpline.com lists free, confidential help in most countries.

What it sends

Only the words you type, and your answer to its one message of questions, are sent to Anthropic. Nothing else from your account or your log is sent. The Privacy Policy has the rest: where the provider processes data, and how long anything is kept. Conversations are kept 30 days on our servers so you can open them again, and you can delete them whenever you like.

  • Your messages and your log are not used to train AI models, by us or by the provider.
  • We do not send your name, email address or anything else that is not needed to answer.

Tell us when it gets it wrong

Every answer has a way to report it. You can also write to liftbound [dot] support [at] gmail [dot] com. We read what you send and use it to make the AI safer.
